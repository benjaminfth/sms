# Brightloop SMS — Product Context

## Product Identity
- **Product Name:** Brightloop SMS (Brightloop Subscription Management System)
- **Domain:** B2B Financial SaaS & Recurring Billing Operations
- **Target Users:**
  1. **ADMIN**: Complete platform control, financial oversight, taxes, staff permissions, manual billing engine runs, reconciliation.
  2. **STAFF**: Day-to-day operations: managing customers, products, plans, quotes, subscriptions, recording payments.
  3. **CUSTOMER**: Self-service customer portal for personal subscriptions, quotes, invoices, payment history, and company profile.

## Key Capabilities
- **Customers**: Multi-contact directory, billing address, tax identification (GSTIN).
- **Catalog**: Products, SKUs, inventory types, recurring plans with billing intervals (weekly, monthly, quarterly, annual).
- **Quotations**: Sales quotes with line items, PDF generation, expiration, customer confirmation, and automatic subscription conversion.
- **Subscriptions**: Recurring lifecycle management (active, trialing, past_due, paused, cancelled, expired), automatic renewals.
- **Billing Engine**: Automated invoicing cycle runs, retry attempts, payment reconciliation.
- **Invoices**: Tax breakdowns (GST 18%), itemized charges, discount applications, credit balance allocation, print-ready document layouts.
- **Payments & Reconciliation**: Payment logging (Card, Bank Transfer, UPI, Cash), unallocated deposit matching, invoice balance clearance.
- **Discounts & Taxes**: Inclusive and exclusive tax rates, fixed and percentage coupons with redemption auditing.

## Tone & Values
- Financial precision, reliability, transparency, efficiency, restrained enterprise elegance.
